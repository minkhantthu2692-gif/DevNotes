Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version: DevNotes is a local-first application. Your notes, snippets, flashcards and preferences are stored in your browser (IndexedDB) and stay on your device. We run no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no external requests unless you choose to enable cloud sync or AI features.

1. Information we collect

DevNotes works without an account. The information involved depends entirely on which optional features you use.

Content you create (always local)

  • Your library: lessons, Markdown notes, code snippets, flashcards, tags, languages, categories and courses you create in the app.
  • Preferences: theme, accent color, typography settings and similar options.

This content is written to IndexedDB in your browser. It is never transmitted anywhere unless you enable sync or take an explicit export action.

Account information (only if you sign in)

Cloud sync and accounts are optional. If you enable them, authentication is handled by Supabase, and we receive:

  • Your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with an identity provider such as Google or GitHub.
  • A stable user identifier used to scope your synced data to your account.
  • The library content you choose to sync.

AI settings (only if you use AI features)

  • A Groq API key that you paste yourself, stored in this browser's local storage (see AI features below).
  • The lesson text you explicitly select as context for a request.

What we do not collect

  • No cookies for tracking or advertising — the app does not use cookies at all.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or clipboard access.

2. Local storage on your device

DevNotes uses two browser storage mechanisms, both under your control:

  • IndexedDB — your entire library, revision history, trash, the sync queue and any imported fonts.
  • localStorage — a few small preferences only (appearance settings, last route, recent searches, and your Groq key if you add one).
  • Service worker cache — cached copies of the app's own files (HTML, CSS, icons) so it loads instantly and works offline. This cache never contains your notes.

Clearing your browser data or using “Erase everything” in Settings removes this information from your device. Exports you download never leave your device automatically.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to operate the features you actually use:

  • To provide the app locally: editing, search, flashcard review, import/export and offline use.
  • To authenticate you and keep your synced data isolated to your account, if you sign in.
  • To synchronize your library across your own devices, if you enable sync.
  • To process the context you select with the AI provider you configured, if you use AI features.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, marketing or building behavioral profiles, and we do not sell it.

4. Third-party services

The app itself is hosted as a static site (GitHub Pages). Beyond that, the only external services that ever see data are the ones you opt into:

GitHub Pages (hosting)

The application files are served by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard technical request data (such as IP address and user agent) to deliver the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Supabase (optional — sync and accounts)

If you enable cloud sync, your account identity and synced library data are stored in a Supabase project. Access is enforced by database Row Level Security so that your data can only be read or written by your own authenticated session. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Google and GitHub sign-in (optional)

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, those providers share your basic profile (name, email and profile picture) with Supabase to create your session. We do not receive your password. Your use of these providers is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement, respectively.

5. Optional AI features

AI assistance is entirely opt-in and disabled until you configure it. In the default (bring-your-own-key) mode:

  • You supply your own Groq API key; it is stored only in this browser's local storage and is sent from your browser directly to api.groq.com — it never passes through our servers.
  • Only the content you explicitly select is sent, capped at a small amount of lesson text per request. Your whole library is never uploaded.
  • AI-generated drafts are never applied to your notes without your explicit confirmation.

Please note: a key stored in a browser is protected from other users of the app, but not from someone with access to your own browser profile. Use a key with appropriate limits, and revoke it any time at console.groq.com/keys. Groq's handling of your requests is described in Groq's Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Data is shared only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers you opt into — Supabase (sync/accounts) and Groq (AI), strictly to provide the features you enabled.
  • With your consent — for example, when you download an export file or share content yourself.
  • Legal requirements — when required by applicable law, legal process or a valid government request.
  • Protection of rights — to protect the service, its users or others from harm, fraud or abuse.

7. Data retention

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it — individually, via Trash, or with “Erase everything” in Settings. We cannot and do not access it.
  • Synced data and your account are retained while your account is active. You can delete individual records in the app at any time; to have your account and all server-side copies of your synced data removed, use the contact details below and we will process the request.
  • Backups of the sync database rotate on the hosting provider's standard backup schedule and expire within a few weeks.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability — export your entire library at any time from Settings → Account & data as a versioned JSON file or a ZIP with Markdown mirrors. Exports download straight to your device.
  • Correction — edit any of your content whenever you like.
  • Deletion — delete single items, purge the Trash, or erase all local data from Settings.
  • Opt out of sync — sync can be suspended at any time; suspending or signing out never deletes your local library.
  • Opt out of AI — remove your key in Settings → AI to disable AI features completely.
  • Right to complaint — if you are in a region with data-protection rights (such as the EU/EEA or UK), you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any right regarding data held on our sync service, contact us using the details in Contact.

9. Security

  • Synced data is protected by database Row Level Security policies that scope every read and write to your authenticated user — the server enforces ownership, not the interface.
  • All network traffic uses TLS encryption, both in transit to Supabase and to the AI provider.
  • Markdown rendering is sanitized, and saved content is never executed as code.
  • No secret keys ship in the application; the only shared secret (for optional proxy-based AI) lives server-side in infrastructure, never in frontend code.

If you believe you have found a security issue, please report it responsibly through the GitHub repository (see Contact).

10. Children's privacy

DevNotes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

DevNotes is a static application and the optional Supabase project may be hosted in a country different from yours. If you enable sync, your data is processed there by Supabase under its privacy policy and data-processing terms. By enabling sync, you acknowledge such transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced in the project repository. Continued use of DevNotes after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
