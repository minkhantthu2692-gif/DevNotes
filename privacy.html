Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

DevNotes ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use DevNotes, you may provide:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • User Content: Notes, code snippets, tags, and other content you create within the application.
  • Settings and Preferences: Your application preferences, theme choices, font selections, and other configuration data.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the application, features used, and error logs.
  • Local Storage: Data stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB for offline functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve DevNotes
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Synchronize your notes across devices
  • Provide AI-powered features when you choose to use them
  • Respond to your requests, questions, and feedback
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

DevNotes is designed as an offline-first application. Your notes and content are primarily stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. This data remains under your control and is not automatically transmitted to our servers.

Cloud Storage

When you enable synchronization:

  • Your data is stored in Supabase's secure PostgreSQL database
  • File attachments may be stored in Supabase Storage
  • All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols
  • Data at rest is encrypted using Supabase's encryption standards

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only access their own data
  • Secure authentication via Google OAuth 2.0
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest

4. Third-Party Services

DevNotes integrates with the following third-party services:

Google OAuth

We use Google OAuth for authentication. When you sign in with Google, you grant us access to your basic profile information (name, email, profile picture). Google's use of your information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Supabase

We use Supabase for backend services including authentication, database, and file storage. Supabase's data handling practices are governed by their Privacy Policy.

Groq AI (Optional)

When you choose to use AI features and provide your own API key:

  • Your selected content is sent to Groq's API for processing
  • We do not store or log AI-generated content on our servers
  • Groq's data practices are governed by their Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share specific information
  • Service Providers: With third-party services that help us operate DevNotes (Supabase, Groq)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, legal process, or government request
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, and that of our users

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can view and export all your data from within the application
  • Correction: You can edit your notes and content at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual notes or your entire account
  • Data Portability: You can export your data in standard formats (JSON, ZIP)
  • Opt-Out: You can disable synchronization or AI features at any time

To exercise these rights or for questions about your data, contact us at the email address below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account:

  • Your data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days
  • Backups containing your data will be purged according to our backup retention schedule (maximum 90 days)
  • Local data stored on your device will remain until you clear your browser data

8. Children's Privacy

DevNotes is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information as quickly as possible.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws different from your country. By using DevNotes, you consent to the transfer of your information to our facilities and service providers as described in this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Providing notice within the application for significant changes

Your continued use of DevNotes after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
